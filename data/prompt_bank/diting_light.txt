DiTing is a large-scale Chinese earthquake benchmark dataset comprising 1,288,973 earthquake events from China and its surrounding regions from 2013 to 2020, totaling 3,112,194 three-component (vertical, north-south, east-west) seismic waveform data. All waveform data were recorded at a sampling rate of 50 Hz. The dataset provides rich seismological labels for each waveform, including P-wave and S-wave arrival times, magnitude, source azimuth, epicentral distance, and signal-to-noise ratio, among other parameters.
